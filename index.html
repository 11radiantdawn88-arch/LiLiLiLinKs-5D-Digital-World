What “5D” means for LiLiLinKs

Your 5D concept is the product vision—not a downgrade to a standard 3D world. The experience can combine multiple layers of interaction while being developed in achievable stages.

A feeling of being together through spatial scenes, avatars, sound, and responsive environments.

Build plan: from starter to real platform

This package now includes separate module pages, a local single-player game, a LiLiID Supabase Auth front end, a proposed database schema, and server-side Stripe Checkout scaffolding. These pieces still require provider configuration and testing; live email delivery, live meetings, multiplayer, and live payments are not enabled.
